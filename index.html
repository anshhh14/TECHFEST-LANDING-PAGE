01 · Briefing

Not a conference. A campus that runs on adrenaline for 72 hours straight.

Techfest started as a handful of engineering students trying to prove a point about what a student-run festival could look like. It's grown into the kind of event where a fifteen-year-old's robotics prototype ends up in the same hall as a fireside chat with a space-agency scientist — and nobody finds that strange anymore. Every corridor is a different discipline: aerial robotics on one side, a 48-hour hackathon on the other, a planetarium dome pitched on the lawn in between.

27+years running
150+events & competitions
60+countries represented
